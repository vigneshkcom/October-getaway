    Why the Port place wins

    💰

    It is the cheapest bed of the three

    $606 against $800 and $900. Even after the longer drive, Port comes out $59 per person under Huskisson and $34 under Currarong.

    🛁

    Two bathrooms, not one

    The only one of the three with a second bathroom. Four adults, one shower, two mornings — this is the difference you actually feel on the trip.

    🏠

    A whole house instead of a flat

    Private driveway, big backyard, deck for a barbecue and afternoon drinks. The other two are apartments.

    ⭐

    A host with a track record

    Belinda has hosted 13 years at 4.75 stars. The Huskisson place is a brand new listing with no reviews and a first-year host.

    🧹

    Properly kitted out

    32 amenities against Huskisson's 14: washer and dryer, aircon, bathtub, full new kitchen, Netflix. Plus an 11am checkout instead of 10am.

    Hideaway Festival

    Port Macquarie Hastings Council turns two of the town's laneways into a street party with live music, art, food and drinks. It's back for a second year after last November's first edition, which had street art, food stalls and local craft brewers.
